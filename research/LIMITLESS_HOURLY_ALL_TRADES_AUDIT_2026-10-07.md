## Repeated failure patterns

1. **The discovery gain is concentrated.** Its two largest wins returned +51.071 and +35.154, jointly +86.224. Removing either the largest leaves discovery at −27.985; removing both leaves **−63.139** across 17 trades. This is outcome sensitivity, not proof that these wins were invalid. The holdout loses in all six entry-hour clusters: −16.242, −6.814, −17.893, −8.149, −9.740, −15.541. BTC and ETH in the same hour are correlated; ten fills are only six time clusters.
2. **The price-based mean-reversion selection repeatedly failed.** `constant50` assigns 0.5 to both sides and therefore always chooses the sufficiently cheap side against the half-hour BTC/ETH move when a fill exists. It won 32/132 and lost −54.683. MODEL also bought **against** that move in 17/29 fills, winning 2/17 and netting −41.032 overall. In discovery this subgroup made +8.644 because two low-price wins paid heavily; in holdout 0/6 and −49.676. The 12 momentum-aligned MODEL fills were 8/12, net −10.261: direction alone does not rescue economics because price and stake matter. This is descriptive attribution, not a prospective filter or a claim that every reversal is wrong.
3. **The apparent model edge did not materialize in holdout.** For the ten MODEL fills, mean estimated probability of the purchased side was .4785 versus mean fee adjusted break even .4112. Their model-implied expected PnL was +16.792, realized −74.379; just one of ten paid. In the prior [ten-trade source-book audit](LIMITLESS_HOURLY_STOP_AND_POSTMORTEM_2026-10-07.md), all ten model probabilities exceeded contemporaneous market midpoints, mean gap .0884, while selected-trade Brier was .2257 for the model versus .1585 for market midpoints. That suggests overestimation of relative edge on selected cases; ten trades and six clusters are too few to pin down a stable calibration correction. Comparable decision midpoints for every earlier discovery entry were not independently matched in this audit.
4. **Fees alone cannot explain the holdout loss.** Holding observed execution prices and outcomes fixed, removing the assumed 3% fee improves MODEL holdout by only .366 USDC (−74.379 to −74.013). It improves discovery by 5.848. This is an attribution at the same fills, not a zero-fee rerun: fee removal could change admissible trades. Bad settlement outcomes and paying for the wrong side dominate the holdout losses.
5. **A simple overall MODEL versus control PnL comparison is misleading.** MODEL admitted 29 while constant50 admitted 132, with different entry prices and position sizes. On the nine holdout conditions where both settled, MODEL returned −66.230 versus control −5.040, but six sides agreed and three opposed; their stakes differ. This matched subset is diagnostic, not a randomized strategy comparison. On 18 matching discovery conditions MODEL +14.243 versus control −26.592. The phase reversal reinforces that discovery alone should not be used to promote the model.

No ledger arithmetic discrepancy was found in this pass. The frozen model uses the hour open, a half-hour Binance reference price and recent 1m volatility to estimate a normal-CDF settlement probability; it picks the largest positive modeled net edge among executable YES/NO books. These outcomes implicate **probability/selection economics**, particularly buying apparently cheap counter-move outcomes, rather than a demonstrated settlement decoder or commission accounting defect. The [prior holdout audit](LIMITLESS_HOURLY_STOP_AND_POSTMORTEM_2026-10-07.md) matched all ten results against closed Binance 1h candles and preserved decision book source hashes.

## Next research gate

Keep collection stopped and the frozen holdout intact. Review the calibration of **all decision probabilities**, not only fills, against market midpoints and settlements, then use a genuinely new forward period if a separately versioned probability or selection rule is proposed. Retrospective exclusions based on the losing side, price bin, structure, news or ATR would overfit this sample. Any maker alternative needs queue and actual fill evidence before a PnL comparison. The paper book assumption and six independent holdout entry hours prevent a profitability claim.
