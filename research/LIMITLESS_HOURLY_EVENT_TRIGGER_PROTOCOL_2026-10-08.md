# Limitless hourly event-trigger shadow: frozen diagnostic protocol

Recorded before computing this event-trigger replay on the 96 already SHA256-verified main capture ZIPs. This is a research comparison, not a production rule or a new independent holdout. No new thresholds are tuned.

- Universe: the same 162 hourly-v1 BTC/ETH Limitless condition IDs, their existing discovery/old-holdout labels and UTC-hour clusters. Existing frozen model and its original :30 benchmark remain untouched.
- Starting when a market is prospectively observed FUNDED after its exact hour open, visit **every recorded REST YES-token book in receipt order** through expiry. A WebSocket update alone does not certify full executable depth; it cannot itself be an entry quote. At each book, join only the most recently *received* Binance 1m/1h response. Retain the existing freshness limits (10s/120s), exact hour-open match, 121 contiguous fully closed one-minute bars and current partly formed candle, with the 1m request time as the closed-bar availability cutoff. Ignore invalid observations and keep waiting. Record all considered events and the first eligible opportunity; never pick the most profitable later quote.
- The first eligible opportunity is the first valid book where frozen `probability`, unchanged 3% contract fee, 3% edge hurdle, full visible ask depth and 10 USDC intended gross spend permit either side. If no event qualifies, record NO_TRADE, not a hypothetical fill. If one qualifies, choose the maximum expected-edge side using the frozen tie rule and permanently lock the initial decision.
- Wait the same venue delay (1.5s in the current markets); within 30s take **only the first** subsequent matching book request, including a failed request. Missing/error/late/invalid/insufficient response permanently skips that entry; no retry or retrospective midpoint fill. A fill is still a quote-based paper execution proxy, not an actual order.
- For a successful first paper entry, inspect subsequent recorded valid books whose requests began after the first entry response, in receipt order. Record the **first observed** opposite ask depth able to buy enough gross contracts to match the first leg's net contracts after the same 3% fee, for at most 10 additional USDC and a combined guaranteed binary payout strictly greater than both costs. This is an *availability diagnostic*, not an executed second leg: no invented second-order latency, maker queue priority, replenishment or paired realized PnL. One-sided entry settles according to observed payout and retains its original paper PnL.
- Report denominators (markets, books, valid as-of inputs, eligible opportunities, first failed attempts, entries, later pair-quote availability), phase-separated settled first-leg PnL and UTC-hour clustering. Verify the original :30 same-source calculation where comparable. Previously inspected holdout is descriptive only. A positive backtest cannot promote this candidate without a new frozen prospective evaluation.

The event stream is what the collector observed, not a complete venue tape; absence of a qualifying event means none was seen in these archives. Structural filters, altered fees, maker fills and dynamic re-entry are outside this protocol.
