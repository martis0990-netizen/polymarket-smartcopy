# Limitless hourly Markov-volatility diagnostic, frozen before replay

Paper-only exploratory comparison on the existing 96 SHA256-verified main ZIPs and the published event-trigger ledger. Existing 162 condition IDs and discovery/old-holdout labels remain fixed. The old holdout was repeatedly inspected and is **not** an independent test for this new candidate. Do not alter the running hourly model, threshold, schedule, holdout, fees or live-order permissions.

Model: use only the 121 contiguous fully closed Binance 1m candles that the frozen model had received by each saved **first qualifying event** (its existing 1m response request time is the availability cutoff). Form 120 consecutive close-to-close log returns. Define observed states LOW/HIGH by whether each absolute return is <= or > the median of those 120 absolute returns. Estimate each state's per-minute variance as its mean squared return. Estimate the 2x2 one-step transition matrix from the 119 adjacent state pairs with fixed Laplace +1/+1 smoothing. Starting at the last observed state, forecast the sum of expected one-minute variances across the remaining time to expiry (integer minutes plus a proportional fraction). Preserve zero drift and the exact observed current price and market hour open. Set `p_up = Phi(log(current/open) / sqrt(forecast_variance))`. No fitted directional drift, HMM, threshold optimization or training on settlements.

Selection/evaluation: the saved first event timestamp/book/condition and first delayed execution request are held **identical** to the original event replay. The candidate can say NO_TRADE at that fixed opportunity or choose YES/NO with the unchanged 3% contract fee, 3% edge hurdle, full visible ask depth, 10 USDC gross leg budget, microshare rounding, venue delay and 30s expiry. Use the first previously captured execution request, including errors. Never search a later favorable decision or execution book; no pair leg in this test. Score only observed binary/split payouts. Compare the candidate's Brier/log loss, decisions, paper fills, settled PnL, UTC-hour clusters and top-two concentration against the **same-event** frozen baseline and midpoint forecast. Conditions with no saved first opportunity remain outside the conditional candidate comparison; report that denominator explicitly. Freshness, hour-open identity, book token, raw line hashes and ZIP SHA256 must be checked. Errors are SKIP_INPUT, not manufactured probabilities or fills.

This tests whether a simple observable volatility regime transition changes conditional first-event pricing, not whether a hidden Markov model, a Bonereaper strategy or future profitability is proved. Any apparent improvement on old holdout needs a separately frozen prospective evaluation.
