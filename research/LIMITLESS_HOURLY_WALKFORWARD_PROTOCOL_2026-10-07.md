# Frozen diagnostic: does Binance add value beyond the Limitless book?

Protocol recorded before evaluating the new counterfactual PnL. This is a research-only shadow replay of already collected hourly-v1 public data, not a production strategy, a new collection, or live execution. The old holdout has already been inspected and cannot serve as independent confirmation.

1. Decision inputs are the frozen `paper_decision` and its triggering raw `book` in the 96 SHA-checked main archives. `p_model` is the already saved Binance prediction. `p_book` is the YES best bid/ask midpoint at the **same decision receipt**, used **only as a forecast**. No midpoint trade is permitted.
2. At a decision time `t`, fit one weight `w ∈ [0,1]` to binary log loss of `q=w·p_model+(1−w)·p_book` using **only** prior conditions whose settlement was already observed before `t`. Use all such conditions (YES outcome), not just prior fills, and no PnL optimization, phase-specific fitting, market-specific thresholds or window search. If none have settled, use the fixed starting weight `w=0.5`. BTC/ETH events in the same UTC hour cannot train each other before settlement. Record `w` and training count at every decision.
3. Apply the unchanged paper entry rule at the decision: for each side `chance=q` or `1−q`, buy cap `chance·0.97/1.03`, target gross shares `floor_1e−6(10/cap)`, and require the full target to fit within the visible book levels at cap with total cost at most 10 USDC. Choose the original maximum expected-edge side if both pass. Otherwise NO_TRADE.
4. The 1.5-second venue wait and 30-second expiry begin at that decision. For an eligible side, take the **first** recorded `book` or `request_error` for that exact slug whose request began after eligibility. If it is an error, missing, invalid, after expiry, or lacks target depth at the original cap, SKIP permanently. Never choose a later favorable response. Book receipts are hypothetical taker paper fills, not orders or guaranteed execution; report missing/error attempts distinctly.
5. Use the saved observed binary payout to calculate paper PnL for a hypothetical first-attempt fill, and keep unresolved conditions unscored. Compare decision counts, first-attempt coverage, Brier/log loss, settled PnL, UTC-hour clusters and concentration against frozen MODEL, constant50 and NO_TRADE. Discovery and the previously inspected holdout stay separate; do not sum overlapping variants as independent trades. One or two jackpots are not robust evidence.

This protocol fixes the one candidate and its evaluation method before looking at the candidate's counterfactual profit. A historical positive result would still be exploratory, given the repeatedly inspected old holdout. A failure is reported unchanged; no threshold search follows from it.
