# Hourly multifactor hypothesis: avoid counting the same evidence twice

The frozen hourly forecast is `Phi(log(reference/open) / (sigma_1m × sqrt(minutes remaining)))`. Thus distance from the hourly opening, estimated volatility and time left are **already a single probability signal**. Adding ATR, distance-to-open and time as independent votes would repeat the same information. The Limitless YES book midpoint is a separate *forecast comparator* but not an executable price; previous chronological model/book blending had two losing old-holdout fills. Spread, full ask depth, delay and fee belong to the **execution** screen, not probability calibration.

One potentially different piece of information is the recent **path** of Binance prices: two markets can have the same current distance from opening and different last-five-minute direction. As a descriptive check, for each original 30-minute condition with a usable first book, compute the log return across five **fully closed** one-minute candles, divided by the frozen `sigma_1m × sqrt(5)`. Multiply that signed feature by either observed YES outcome minus market midpoint or outcome minus model probability. Positive covariance would motivate (but not validate) a positive momentum correction. Source candle request/receipt and book line are preserved in [machine evidence](evidence/limitless_hourly_momentum_increment_2026-10-07.json). This is **not** a fitted multi-factor forecast or a trading rule.

The sign flips between periods and both cluster intervals cross zero. This does **not** show stable added predictive information from a five-minute momentum vote. The calculation is a marginal residual covariance; correlation with the existing probability inputs is not removed, so even a stable sign would require a prospective conditional-score test. The 16 strict-first minute-30 missing-current-candle skips and two unresolved final conditions are outside this scored sample; its selection is not a new target population for profit estimates.

**Next candidate specification, if pursued:** start with the venue midpoint and the existing Binance probability, then allow at most **one** independently timestamped path/structure feature with a written mathematical definition. Fit/calibrate by proper score using only payouts observed before each UTC-hour decision; test against midpoint alone and the prior two-source blend on identical conditions; apply unchanged executable asks, fees and first-request policy in a separate economic report. Freeze the feature and regularization before any future untouched period.
