## Market and target

Only BTC/USD and ETH/USD **15-minute CLOB** Up/Down, a UTC quarter-hour open, expiration exactly 900 seconds later, USDC collateral with six decimals, unambiguous YES/NO token identities, finite positive `metadata.openPrice`, and enabled market-specific Chainlink BTC/USD or ETH/USD **60-second TWAP** stream. Verify the description's feed, opening/expiration times and comparison rule, and do not accept a different oracle or unknown/tolerated fallback as a binary result without observed payout. Price to Beat is the market's recorded TWAP at open. The observed RESOLVED market's `winningOutcomeIndex` or explicit valid split `payoutNumerators` determines paper payout; a Binance close never labels this market. The captured oracle 1m arrays do not independently reconstruct the exact boundary stream report. [Limitless resolution rules](https://docs.limitless.exchange/user-guide/market-resolution).

## One causal decision per condition

- Window: **open + 7m30s to open + 8m30s**, inclusive. Take the **first completed oracle request** for this slug whose response or request-error is received in this window, including a failed first request, and require a known `requested_at` not after receipt. A successful response must have `source=chainlink` and exact asset/market slug. A failed or invalid first request is an immutable SKIP; later success cannot replace it. No completed attempt => MISSED_WINDOW.
- From that response, use only contiguous 1m candle closes whose `timestamp + 60s <= request_start`. Require **121** consecutive closed candles to obtain **120** log returns. Require finite positive USD values after dividing the 1e18-padded raw price by 1e18; no duplicate/conflicting minute or missing minute in the warmup. Last closed candle end must precede response receipt by **at most 120s** and cannot be in the future. Otherwise SKIP. This 120s stale gate is inherited as a conservative operational bound; it is not selected from the 15m retrospective PnL.
- Zero-drift probability `p_up = Phi(log(last_closed_TWAP / Price_to_Beat) / (sample_std_120_log_returns * sqrt((expiry - last_closed_minute_end)/60)))`. Require finite positive volatility and remaining horizon. The horizon starts at the **stale reference candle end**, while the decision can only happen after its response receipt. Record both source time and receipt.
- The first **subsequently requested** REST book or book request-error (`requested_at >= oracle_response_receipt`) received inside the same 60s window is the sole decision attempt; error/invalid source/identity/book => SKIP, no favorable retry. YES asks come from YES asks; NO asks from `1 - YES bids`, size in 1e6 share units. Check token ID, crossed/empty books and finite price/size. No latest-price backfill.

## Paper execution and accounting

Compare this model with an independent constant-50% control on the same decision book. Each has a separate initial **100 USDC** virtual cash account at the prospective start; no borrowing, one entry per condition, no legacy carry, no inter-account funding and no mid-trade management. Per condition and variant: conservative **3% buy fee deducted from shares**, **3% minimum modeled return** on spent USDC, **10 USDC maximum cash spend**, six-decimal shares. For each YES/NO side, limit price `p_side * 0.97 / 1.03`; size cap `floor_6(10 / limit_price)`. Require that entire size be available in the decision book at or below the cap and within the 10 USDC budget. Choose the side with highest modeled net expected return, using the existing hourly-v1 deterministic side tie-break; otherwise NO_TRADE. No parameter sweep or payoff-based change to the decision.

A decision-book quote is **never** an execution. Lock side, size and max price. After the market's finite `takerDelayMs` plus one second, inspect the **first subsequently requested** book only; its request must start after eligibility and its receipt must be within 30s of decision and before expiration. Failed request, absent/malformed book, insufficient full depth, changed token, price bound or budget failure => SKIP irrevocably. For a valid simulated fill debit its actual depth-weighted cost and credit `gross_shares * 0.97` net claims. No partial fills, midpoint fills, retries, maker queue, rebates, gas or assumed on-chain redemption. Record book/request/receipt/source fingerprints, selected quote, entry cost, quantity, account cash and immutable first-attempt outcome.

Hold claims until a subsequent observed RESOLVED record at/after expiration. Winning YES/NO share pays 1/0; a valid two-sided split pays the explicit proportions. `net_shares * payout - cost` is settled PnL. PENDING claims retain cost basis and are excluded from settled returns; lack of resolution is UNKNOWN, not zero payoff. No trade contributes 0 to condition-level cash PnL. Run archive-level reconciliation of account cash, all debits/payouts, condition identity and cumulative checkpoint lineage before reporting; conflict => UNRECONCILED, report null.

## Evaluation gate

Never sum cumulative checkpoint reports. A minimum **60 resolved scored conditions and 60 distinct quarter-hour clusters** plus >=90% relevant observation coverage is a *coverage review*, not evidence of profit. Bootstrap uncertainty by quarter-hour cluster and show the same-time BTC/ETH dependence. Quote/fill availability remains an approximation: REST cache, competing takers, queue position and real matching are absent. No capital deployment follows automatically.
