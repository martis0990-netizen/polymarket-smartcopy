# Limitless hourly hidden Markov volatility shadow: frozen protocol

Recorded before examining HMM outcomes. One fixed two-state zero-mean Gaussian HMM for one-minute log-return volatility, paper-only on the same 96 SHA256-verified archives, 162 condition IDs and 154 already observed first qualifying event books. Old holdout repeatedly inspected; this is exploratory and cannot become an independent holdout. No threshold, fee, schedule, capital, production or live-order change.

- Inputs: exactly the 121 contiguous fully closed one-minute candles available at the saved first-event book receipt, under the archived Binance response request-time availability cutoff. Compute 120 close-to-close log returns. The current partially formed candle is reference price only. Exact hour open, current source freshness, slug, yes token and timestamp conditions remain those of the existing event replay.
- Hidden states LOW/HIGH emit independent zero-mean Gaussian returns with state-specific variance. Deterministic initialization: low variance = 0.25 times the 120-return mean squared return; high = 4 times; transition self-probability 0.9 for both states; initial state probabilities 0.5 each. Variances constrained to [0.05,20] times the as-of mean squared return.
- Exactly 20 scaled forward-backward EM iterations. Transition counts receive fixed pseudocounts [[4,1],[1,4]]; each state's variance update receives 10 pseudo observations at the as-of mean squared return. One initialization, no restarts, no fitted directional means, no PnL or outcome labels, no choice among hidden-state counts or hyperparameters.
- Refilter all 120 closed returns with fitted as-of parameters. From the final filtered state distribution, propagate the two-state chain across remaining fractional minutes, sum expected conditional variances, and use the unchanged zero-drift binary formula Phi(log(current/open)/sqrt(integrated variance)). No future candle or settlement enters model fitting.
- At each of the same 154 first event opportunities, compare HMM p with frozen p on binary Brier/log loss and recompute YES/NO/NO_TRADE with unchanged 3% contract buy fee, 3% entry hurdle, full visible ask depth, 10 USDC leg budget, 1.5s venue wait, 30s expiry and **same first archived delayed request**, including errors. No second leg and no new event-time search. The eight conditions without original first opportunity remain outside this conditional candidate comparison. Settlement uses the observed saved payout; unobserved payout remains unscored.
- Report both discovery and repeatedly inspected old holdout, fills, net PnL, top-two concentration, UTC-hour clusters, probability deltas, hidden-state persistence and input failures. A positive old result would remain exploratory; no hidden tuning or strategy promotion.

This is a volatility-only HMM, not a model of direction, order flow or Bonereaper's private decisions. The scientific hypothesis is that state persistence changes the remaining-hour variance enough to improve probability and execution economics.
