That timing is after a 15-minute market closes; the outcome is also a different oracle quantity. ## Exploratory oracle forecast (discovery data only)

The reproducible offline [audit](limitless_15m_audit.py) also evaluates the **forecast component only**, using these already viewed October 3–4 outcomes. It is an adaptation of the hourly zero-drift normal formula to the Limitless Chainlink candle series, **not** a replay of the frozen hourly strategy and not a 15m paper executor. For each condition with both diagnostic-window receipts and a later observed result, take the **first oracle response received** in the diagnostic window; accept only one-minute candles fully closed before its **request start**. Use the latest closed candle's close, the market's stored Chainlink Price to Beat, and sample standard deviation of the 120 latest consecutive log returns. The forecast horizon is **from the end of that stale reference candle to expiry**; the candle is unavailable until its response receipt. Score the observed binary winner only after resolution. An accompanying received book is required for the coverage subset, but no quote is used in the probability calculation or called a fill.

Result: **186 conditions / 96 quarter-hour clusters**; Brier score **0.17481** versus **0.25000** for the constant-50% forecast, directional favourite accuracy **76.34%**. A fixed-seed, 5,000-resample cluster bootstrap of the descriptive Brier improvement gives roughly **0.0375–0.1079**. These are retrospective discovery summaries: we selected the diagnostic window after observing the sample's coverage, so that interval is **not a prospective confidence claim**. BTC and ETH in the same quarter-hour are correlated. Source values are 1e18-padded and decoded to quoted USD for the calculation.

This result suggests there is directional information in the oracle path. It **does not establish an executable edge or profitability**: no 15m trading decisions, first post-delay attempts, fees, depth-adjusted entries, funding/management or matched settlement PnL have been simulated. The reference often lags receipt by about 105 seconds, and the exact boundary stream report is absent from archived oracle candle arrays. A prospective 15m paper version must fix its decision, staleness/unknown gates and economics **before** its separate untouched evaluation period; the existing hourly holdout and running collection remain unchanged.

Focused verification: the offline script compiled and reconciled all 29 artifact ZIP hashes. Re-running it on the first 28 main segments yielded 179 forecast rows; all 179 common forecast probabilities were byte-for-byte unchanged after including segment 29. No conflicting market condition/Price to Beat/feed identity, mismatched book YES token, winner-price direction or conflicting winners were found in this sample. This checks a useful prefix property but does not establish exchange fillability.
