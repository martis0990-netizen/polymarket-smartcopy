# Initial Research Universe

This document records the starting hypothesis universe, not permanent product scope.

## Primary market family

Priority 1:

- BTC Up/Down
- ETH Up/Down
- later SOL/BNB if active/liquid enough

Prefer short horizons (5m/15m/1h/4h where available) because they provide repeated independent decisions, active CLOB microstructure and an external crypto reference market for validation.

## Primary wallet class

Start with high-frequency, repeatable, category-specialized crypto Up/Down traders. Bonereaper-class activity is the first research target because it appears to provide a large number of repeated short-horizon decisions.

Important: this is a discovery prior, not proof of skill or copyability. The wallet must pass Stage 1–3 gates like every other source.

## Secondary wallet/market classes

After the crypto Up/Down proof cycle:

- high-frequency sports wallets (swisstony-class)
- active esports wallets / repeatable match markets

These are useful for systematic behavior research but lack the clean external BTC/ETH reference available to crypto markets.

## Exclusions for the first dataset/research cycle

Do not prioritize:

- near-resolved `0.001/0.999` markets
- long-dated low-update politics/geopolitics
- niche markets with very low trade count
- frozen books
- markets selected only because nominal liquidity is high

## Market capture prefilter

A candidate market should pass a lightweight activity gate before collection:

- recent trades present
- regular book updates
- both sides populated
- spread not permanently frozen
- price not pinned near 0/1 for nearly all observations
- enough remaining lifetime for research

Market selection should optimize research information density, not raw market count.
